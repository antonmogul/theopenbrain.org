## The Loewi widget in the pane (fixed in this PR)

Reproduced on production, where `20261013010000_history_loewi_beside_text` is already live by hand (see `docs/production-sql.md`): beside "The experiment he dashed off to perform…" the frame reports its own document height, 910 px at 1440×900 and 871 px at 1280×800, inside a pane of 764 / 664 px, so the step buttons sit at y = 805 / 747 and the Source line is under the timeline. The widget sized its drawing from `100vh`, which is the frame, not the pane.

After the fix: `IllustrationWidget` measures its stage and provides the height; `UploadedWidgetEmbed` passes it to `WidgetFrame` as `maxHeight`; the frame never grows past it and tells the widget through the bridge (`OB.maxHeight`, `--ob-max-height`, `html[data-ob-fit]`, from the first paint). The Loewi file lays itself out for that height, so at 1440×900 the frame is 777 px with the drawing, caption, transport and "Next step" all inside it (story `Chapter/Illustrations/IllustrationWidget › Loewi in the pane`, screenshot verified at 1440×900 and 1280×800). Until `20261021030000_history_loewi_widget_fit` is pushed, production's copy of the file is shown shrunk whole to the pane (84% at 1440×900), which is what the deployed code does tonight. Inline bands and breakouts are unchanged.

## Two more things fixed in this PR, found on the walk

**Every jump stopped at the opener (desktop and phone, both chapters, production and the local build).** Clicking a TOC entry, a top-bar section, a timeline bar or a map item scrolled about 600 px and stopped. Cause: `setJumpHistory` in `ChapterView` adds a history entry for the jump with `router.push({ ...route, force: true })` (OPENBRAIN-138/139, so that Back returns); `ChapterView`'s chapter-load watcher was `watch(() => [route.params.number, route.params.slug, route.query.courseId], …)`, and a getter that returns a new array fires on every navigation, the forced same-route push included, so the whole chapter was torn down and reloaded 30 ms into the jump. The watcher now watches one string, so the chapter stays; and `scrollToY` in `helper/readerJump.js` watches a landing jump every 250 ms and scrolls on if the page sits still short of the target (a ScrollTrigger refresh a second later had been putting the page back with no `scrollend` to resume from). Verified on the dev server: the opener's "Failures in comparative neuroanatomy" entry lands with its heading 80 px below the top at 1440×900, Back returns to the place left (807 → 8318 → 807), a map jump does the same, and closing the section menu doesn't move the page (H3). Unit tests cover the watchdog; the view suite still passes.

**Two editor tests are in the live text.** History's first paragraph ends "…our modern understanding of the brain. THis is now editable" (saved from the editor on 9 Oct, 13:48), under the Oliver Sacks quote, the first thing a reader sees after the opener; and the Retina's first section is titled "Story of the eye (EDIT)" (24 Sep), which the opener TOC and the top bar's section menu show. `20261021040000_editor_test_artefacts.sql` removes both (PGlite test `editorTestArtefacts.sql.test.js`). Until it is pushed they are on screen; the creator editor can also fix them by hand (two saves) if the push isn't possible before 17:00.
